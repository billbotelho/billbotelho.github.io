Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Gmail connector is a private, personal tool used only by its owner, Bill Botelho, to access his own Gmail accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the tool has read-only access to the owner's Gmail messages, labels and attachments. It cannot send, change or delete email.

How the data is used

Email is read only when the owner asks for it, to help him search and review his own mail. Content he requests may be processed by the AI assistant he uses to answer his requests. Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
